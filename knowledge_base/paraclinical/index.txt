Para‑Clinical Knowledge Index

Subjects: Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Forensic Medicine, Community Medicine (PSM)
Use: MBBS 2nd year, NEET advanced topics.

Suggested coverage:
- Pathology: cellular injury, inflammation, neoplasia, system pathology
- Microbiology: bacteriology, virology, mycology, parasitology; immunity; lab diagnosis
- Pharmacology: MOA, indications, dosing, ADRs, interactions
- Forensic: medico‑legal aspects, injuries, toxicology basics
- PSM: epidemiology, screening, immunization, programs
