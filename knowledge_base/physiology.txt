# Human Physiology Knowledge Base

## Cardiovascular Physiology
The cardiovascular system maintains circulation through coordinated heart contractions and blood vessel regulation.

### Cardiac Cycle
1. Atrial Systole: Atria contract, pushing blood into ventricles
2. Ventricular Systole: Ventricles contract, pumping blood out
3. Diastole: Heart relaxes, chambers fill with blood

### Blood Pressure Regulation
- Baroreceptors in carotid sinus and aortic arch detect pressure changes
- Sympathetic nervous system increases heart rate and vasoconstriction
- Parasympathetic nervous system decreases heart rate

## Respiratory Physiology
Gas exchange occurs through diffusion across the alveolar-capillary membrane.

### Breathing Mechanics
- Inspiration: Diaphragm contracts, thoracic cavity expands, air flows in
- Expiration: Diaphragm relaxes, thoracic cavity contracts, air flows out

### Gas Exchange
- Oxygen diffuses from alveoli to blood
- Carbon dioxide diffuses from blood to alveoli
- Partial pressures drive diffusion

## Renal Physiology
The kidneys maintain fluid and electrolyte balance through filtration, reabsorption, and secretion.

### Nephron Function
- Glomerular Filtration: Filters blood plasma
- Tubular Reabsorption: Returns needed substances to blood
- Tubular Secretion: Removes additional waste products

### Urine Formation
- Filtration: Creates glomerular filtrate
- Reabsorption: Returns water and solutes to blood
- Secretion: Adds additional substances to urine

## Endocrine Physiology
Hormones regulate body functions through chemical messengers.

### Major Endocrine Glands
- Pituitary: Master gland, controls other endocrine glands
- Thyroid: Regulates metabolism
- Adrenal: Stress response and electrolyte balance
- Pancreas: Blood glucose regulation

## Nervous System Physiology
Neurons communicate through electrical and chemical signals.

### Action Potential
- Resting potential: -70mV
- Depolarization: Sodium influx
- Repolarization: Potassium efflux
- Refractory period: Prevents backward conduction

### Synaptic Transmission
- Presynaptic neuron releases neurotransmitter
- Neurotransmitter binds to postsynaptic receptors
- Postsynaptic potential generated
