Pre‑Clinical Knowledge Index

Subjects: Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry
Use: MBBS 1st year, NEET foundation and core concepts.

Suggested coverage:
- Anatomy: gross, histology, embryology; surface landmarks
- Physiology: homeostasis, neuro-muscular, cardiac, renal, respiratory, endocrine
- Biochemistry: biomolecules, enzymes, metabolism, vitamins, molecular biology

Study guidance:
- Integrate structure (anatomy) with function (physiology) and pathways (biochemistry).
- Prefer tables, flowcharts, and mnemonics for high‑yield revision.
