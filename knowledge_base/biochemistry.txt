# Medical Biochemistry Knowledge Base

## Carbohydrate Metabolism
Carbohydrates are the primary energy source for the body, broken down through glycolysis, citric acid cycle, and oxidative phosphorylation.

### Glycolysis
- Location: Cytoplasm
- Input: Glucose, 2 ATP
- Output: 2 Pyruvate, 2 NADH, 2 ATP (net)
- Rate-limiting enzyme: Phosphofructokinase-1

### Citric Acid Cycle (Krebs Cycle)
- Location: Mitochondrial matrix
- Input: Acetyl-CoA
- Output: 3 NADH, 1 FADH2, 1 ATP, 2 CO2
- Key enzymes: Citrate synthase, Isocitrate dehydrogenase

### Oxidative Phosphorylation
- Location: Inner mitochondrial membrane
- Process: Electron transport chain creates proton gradient
- ATP synthase uses gradient to produce ATP
- Yield: ~30-32 ATP per glucose molecule

## Protein Metabolism
Proteins are broken down into amino acids, which can be used for energy or protein synthesis.

### Amino Acid Catabolism
- Deamination removes amino group
- Carbon skeleton enters metabolic pathways
- Urea cycle removes nitrogen waste

### Protein Synthesis
- Transcription: DNA to mRNA
- Translation: mRNA to protein
- Ribosomes coordinate tRNA and amino acids

## Lipid Metabolism
Lipids provide energy storage and membrane structure.

### Fatty Acid Oxidation
- Beta-oxidation breaks down fatty acids
- Location: Mitochondrial matrix
- Produces acetyl-CoA for citric acid cycle

### Lipid Synthesis
- Fatty acid synthase complex
- Location: Cytoplasm
- Requires NADPH and acetyl-CoA

## Enzyme Kinetics
Enzymes catalyze biochemical reactions with specific kinetics.

### Michaelis-Menten Kinetics
- V = Vmax[S]/(Km + [S])
- Km: Substrate concentration at half-maximal velocity
- Vmax: Maximum velocity

### Enzyme Regulation
- Allosteric regulation: Conformational changes
- Covalent modification: Phosphorylation/dephosphorylation
- Competitive inhibition: Competes with substrate
- Non-competitive inhibition: Binds to different site

## Vitamins and Coenzymes
Essential micronutrients that function as coenzymes.

### Water-Soluble Vitamins
- B-complex vitamins: Coenzyme precursors
- Vitamin C: Antioxidant, collagen synthesis
- Folate: DNA synthesis, cell division

### Fat-Soluble Vitamins
- Vitamin A: Vision, immune function
- Vitamin D: Calcium absorption, bone health
- Vitamin E: Antioxidant
- Vitamin K: Blood clotting
