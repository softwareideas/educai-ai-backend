# Medical Pathology Knowledge Base

## Cellular Injury and Adaptation
Cells respond to stress through various adaptive mechanisms or progress to injury and death.

### Types of Cellular Adaptation
- Hypertrophy: Increase in cell size
- Hyperplasia: Increase in cell number
- Atrophy: Decrease in cell size
- Metaplasia: Change in cell type
- Dysplasia: Abnormal cell growth

### Causes of Cellular Injury
- Hypoxia: Reduced oxygen supply
- Chemical agents: Toxins, drugs
- Physical agents: Trauma, temperature
- Infectious agents: Bacteria, viruses
- Immunologic reactions: Autoimmune diseases

## Inflammation
Inflammation is the body's response to injury or infection.

### Acute Inflammation
- Vascular changes: Vasodilation, increased permeability
- Cellular changes: Neutrophil infiltration
- Chemical mediators: Histamine, prostaglandins, cytokines
- Cardinal signs: Redness, heat, swelling, pain

### Chronic Inflammation
- Persistent stimulus
- Mononuclear cell infiltration
- Tissue destruction and repair
- Granuloma formation

## Neoplasia
Neoplasia refers to abnormal cell growth that may be benign or malignant.

### Characteristics of Cancer Cells
- Uncontrolled proliferation
- Loss of differentiation
- Invasion of surrounding tissue
- Metastasis to distant sites

### Carcinogenesis
- Initiation: DNA damage
- Promotion: Cell proliferation
- Progression: Malignant transformation
- Oncogenes and tumor suppressor genes

## Cardiovascular Pathology
Diseases affecting the heart and blood vessels.

### Atherosclerosis
- Plaque formation in arterial walls
- Risk factors: Hypertension, hyperlipidemia, smoking
- Complications: Myocardial infarction, stroke

### Heart Failure
- Inability to pump blood effectively
- Systolic vs diastolic dysfunction
- Compensatory mechanisms: Hypertrophy, dilation

## Respiratory Pathology
Diseases affecting the lungs and airways.

### Pneumonia
- Infection of lung parenchyma
- Bacterial, viral, fungal causes
- Consolidation and inflammation

### Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD)
- Emphysema: Destruction of alveoli
- Chronic bronchitis: Airway inflammation
- Smoking is primary cause

## Renal Pathology
Diseases affecting the kidneys.

### Glomerulonephritis
- Inflammation of glomeruli
- Proteinuria, hematuria
- Various causes: Immune, infectious

### Acute Kidney Injury
- Rapid decline in kidney function
- Prerenal, intrarenal, postrenal causes
- Oliguria, elevated creatinine
