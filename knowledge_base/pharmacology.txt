# Medical Pharmacology Knowledge Base

## Pharmacokinetics
The study of how the body affects drugs (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion).

### Absorption
- Route of administration affects absorption
- Oral: First-pass metabolism in liver
- Intravenous: Direct entry into circulation
- Factors: pH, blood flow, surface area

### Distribution
- Drug binding to plasma proteins
- Volume of distribution (Vd)
- Blood-brain barrier penetration
- Tissue binding and storage

### Metabolism
- Phase I: Oxidation, reduction, hydrolysis
- Phase II: Conjugation reactions
- Cytochrome P450 enzymes
- Drug interactions affecting metabolism

### Excretion
- Renal elimination: Glomerular filtration, tubular secretion
- Hepatic elimination: Bile excretion
- Half-life: Time for 50% drug elimination

## Pharmacodynamics
The study of how drugs affect the body (mechanism of action, dose-response).

### Drug Receptors
- Agonists: Activate receptors
- Antagonists: Block receptor activation
- Partial agonists: Partial activation
- Inverse agonists: Opposite effect

### Dose-Response Relationships
- Therapeutic index: TD50/ED50
- Potency: Amount needed for effect
- Efficacy: Maximum effect achievable
- Side effects vs therapeutic effects

## Cardiovascular Pharmacology

### Antihypertensive Drugs
- ACE inhibitors: Block angiotensin conversion
- Beta-blockers: Block sympathetic stimulation
- Calcium channel blockers: Block calcium entry
- Diuretics: Increase sodium excretion

### Antiarrhythmic Drugs
- Class I: Sodium channel blockers
- Class II: Beta-blockers
- Class III: Potassium channel blockers
- Class IV: Calcium channel blockers

## Antimicrobial Pharmacology

### Antibiotics
- Penicillins: Cell wall synthesis inhibition
- Cephalosporins: Similar to penicillins
- Macrolides: Protein synthesis inhibition
- Fluoroquinolones: DNA gyrase inhibition

### Antiviral Drugs
- Acyclovir: Viral DNA polymerase inhibition
- Oseltamivir: Neuraminidase inhibition
- Ribavirin: RNA synthesis inhibition

## Central Nervous System Pharmacology

### Anxiolytics and Sedatives
- Benzodiazepines: GABA receptor enhancement
- Barbiturates: GABA receptor enhancement
- Buspirone: Serotonin receptor partial agonist

### Antidepressants
- SSRIs: Selective serotonin reuptake inhibition
- TCAs: Tricyclic antidepressants
- MAOIs: Monoamine oxidase inhibition

## Endocrine Pharmacology

### Diabetes Management
- Insulin: Glucose uptake promotion
- Metformin: Decreased hepatic glucose production
- Sulfonylureas: Insulin secretion stimulation
- Thiazolidinediones: Insulin sensitivity improvement

### Thyroid Drugs
- Levothyroxine: Thyroid hormone replacement
- Methimazole: Thyroid hormone synthesis inhibition
- Propylthiouracil: Thyroid hormone synthesis inhibition
