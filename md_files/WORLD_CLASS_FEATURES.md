### 📊 Comprehensive Question Practice

#### NEET-Style MCQs with Detailed Explanations
```
Q1. Which enzyme is the rate-limiting step in glycolysis?
(A) Hexokinase
(B) Phosphofructokinase-1
(C) Pyruvate kinase
(D) Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase

Answer: (B) Phosphofructokinase-1

✅ Why this is correct:
PFK-1 catalyzes the committed step, converting F6P to F-1,6-BP.
This is the main control point of glycolysis.

❌ Why others are incorrect:
(A) Hexokinase: First step, but not rate-limiting
(C) Pyruvate kinase: Final step, secondary regulation
(D) G6PD: This is in pentose phosphate pathway, not glycolysis

💡 Concept tested: Rate-limiting enzymes
🎯 NEET Pearl: PFK-1 is activated by AMP, F-2,6-BP; inhibited by ATP, citrate
```
